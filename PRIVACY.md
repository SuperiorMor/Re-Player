# Privacy Policy for Re:Player

**Last updated:** October 23, 2026

**Re:Player** ("we", "our", or "the application") is an open-source media player application for Android TV designed to browse and play local UPnP/DLNA media servers and cloud storage files.

## 1. Information Collection and Storage
- **No Personal Data Collected:** Re:Player does not operate any centralized tracking or analytics servers. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal information, viewing habits, or device identifiers.
- **Local Credential Storage:** When connecting your Google Drive account, the OAuth 2.0 authorization tokens received from Google are stored solely on your local Android device inside secure private application storage (`SharedPreferences`). They are never sent to any third-party server or developer servers.

## 2. Use of Google User Data
- **Scopes Requested:** Re:Player requests access to `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file` and basic profile/email for authentication.
- **Data Usage:** Google Drive access is used solely to display your drive folders and stream video and audio files chosen by you on your TV screen.
- **No Third-Party Sharing:** User data obtained through Google APIs is not transferred to any third parties, advertisers, or external services.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
You can disconnect your Google Drive account at any time directly in the Re:Player Settings screen by clicking "Отключить" (Disconnect) or by clearing application data in Android TV system settings. Upon disconnection, all stored tokens are permanently deleted from the device.
